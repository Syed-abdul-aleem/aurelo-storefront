Our story

Built slowly, on purpose.

AURELO began in a two-room Porto atelier with a simple frustration: basics that were not basic enough to last. We now work with six family-run workshops, publish our cost breakdown for every piece, and design each garment backwards from its tenth year of wear — not its first photo.

Natural fibres only

Wool, cotton, silk and linen. No virgin plastic in any garment — and none in the packaging either.

Cut to be repaired

Seam allowances, spare buttons and free repairs for life. A loose stitch should never end a garment.

60-day honest returns

Wear it out into the world. If it is not right after two months, send it back on us.

Small, numbered runs

We make what we can sell without waste. Each run is numbered; when it is gone, it is gone.

Word of mouth

Worn, washed, kept.
